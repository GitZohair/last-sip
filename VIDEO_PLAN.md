# Demo video plan (max 3:00, YouTube, public)

Devpost's eligibility checks: (1) the project visibly **running with Twitch** (real channel chat on screen), (2) **how Codex was used**, said out loud, plus the session ID in the form, (3) the repo link works for judges.

Judges: Twitch engineering (Aiden Wallis, Danny Chi), Twitch Developer Relations (Jonathan Bulava), Twitch creative and content (Declan Pitts, Dominic Galeano, Jarrett Lawson), influencer relations (Steve Marzocca), the Twitch Brand Partnership Studio at Amazon Ads (Brett Bates) and a developer streamer (bashbunni). Show it working with Twitch, show it being funny, mention the zero-setup Twitch connection, and pitch banner planes as a brand-safe sponsor slot.

## Setup before recording
- Screen layout: the game on the left (~70%) and `twitch.tv/popout/<channel>/chat` on the right (~30%), both in frame.
- Connect the game to your own channel (no need to go live). A second account or a friend types in chat. If the account can't chat, connect to a busy live channel (it worked live with #forsen: chatters became customers and rivals) and say so.
