      How to play

      1. 1Load drinks at the BAR in the middle, run them out to the customers on the four islets. Taller tower = bigger tips, and a much wobblier walk.
      2. 2E body-slam. Q or click throws a bomb (grab them off the boards). R fires the CO2 gun: they inflate, float off and pop. F steals a car (or the plane): ram people off the island.
      3. 3Tower fell? It was somebody else's fault. Whoever you blame pays you $10.
      4. 4Your chat runs the sky: !bomb sends a banner plane with their message that bombs the leader, !care drops you a crate of bombs, 1 2 3 votes the next event. Bits, subs, raids and channel points send bigger airstrikes.
      5. 5Every day the arena gets one new rule (moon gravity, bomb day, rush hour...), the same for every streamer. Beat yesterday.
